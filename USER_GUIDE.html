Step-by-Step Guide

How to Use Quran Reel Generator

Five simple steps from choosing a reciter to exporting your finished vertical Quran reel.

Step 1 · Quran Setup

Choose Your Quran Reciter

Pick from 10 renowned reciters (Abdul Basit, Al-Minshawi, Al-Husary, Al-Afasy, Al-Sudais, Al-Shuraim, Al-Muaiqly, Al-Ghamdi, Al-Dosari, and Al-Ajmi). The app downloads and verifies your local audio and Ayah timings automatically.

  • One-Click Setup: Download all 114 Surahs from MP3Quran locally if not already installed.
  • Automatic Verification: Checks every Surah MP3, Quran text, and Ayah timing file automatically.
  • 100% Offline Ready: Once downloaded, everything runs privately on your computer with no internet needed.
Step 2 · Surah & Reel Queue

Select Any Surah & Auto-Rebalancing Queue

Open the themed Select Surah dropdown to jump to any of the 114 Surahs, clearly displaying the Surah number, Arabic name, English name, and Ayah count (286 Ayah / ٢٨٦ آية).

  • Automatic Reel Queue: Long Surahs are neatly split into reel segments aligned to Ayah boundaries.
  • Instant Auto-Rebalancing: Changing a reel's Start or End time automatically updates all surrounding reels with zero errors.
  • Seamless Switching: Click any reel in the queue to preview and edit it instantly.
Step 3 · Precision Audio Waveform

Trim Effortlessly on the Interactive Waveform

Inspect the symmetrical, centered blue waveform with 15-minute subdivisions, clean bottom Ayah numbers, and a refined Full Surah scrubber starting at a comfortable 20% default volume.

  • Draggable S / E Handles: Drag the sage-green S (Start) and orange E (End) handles directly on the waveform, or click the matching Start / End buttons at the playhead.
  • Snap to Closest Pause: Detected pauses appear in soft blue when zoomed in; click Snap to closest Pause for clean cuts that never clip a word.
  • Auto-Saving Ranges: Every boundary adjustment saves automatically and updates the live 9:16 preview immediately.
Step 4 · 9:16 Video Studio & 1-Click Export

Style Your Reel Live & Export in One Click

Play your vertical 9:16 preview right beside the waveform with smooth crossfading background loops, calm text dissolves, and automatic inter-ayah pause hiding — then export directly with zero approval gates.

  • Organized Media & Smooth Loops: Starter backgrounds (Sample1–Sample3) are built in with thumbnail previews, custom upload support, and seamless crossfade looping in both preview and export.
  • Stackable Multi-Effects: Combine fade speed, Zoom/Pan motion, and cinematic color looks (Warm, Golden, Night, Vignette, Soft Focus) with green checkmarks on active choices. Click outside any tray to close it.
  • Typography & Overlays: Customize Size (default Medium), Position, Backdrop, Colour, and toggle Show surah name & Show ayah number (in both Arabic and English).
  • 1-Click Green Export Reel: Always ready — saves your latest audio range & video settings and renders a crisp 720×1280 H.264 + AAC MP4 straight into Exported Reels.
Step 5 · Arabic & English (EN / عربي)

Full English & Arabic Support

Toggle EN / عربي in the top bar anytime. Both the workspace interface and your exported video overlay adapt seamlessly:

  • Arabic Mode (عربي): Renders authentic KFGQPC Uthmani Hafs v18 Arabic script with optional Arabic Surah name and ornate Ayah markers ﴿١﴾.
  • English Mode (EN): Pairs the verified Arabic verse with its complete English translation, plus both Arabic & English Surah names and Ayah numbers.
  • Scripture Integrity: Arabic verses are never truncated, altered, or AI-rewritten.
Under the Hood

How Everything Works Locally

Engineered for scriptural accuracy, smooth visual craft, and 100% local privacy.

🖥️

100% Local Processing

Runs directly on your machine using Python/Flask + React + FFmpeg. No accounts, no cloud uploads, no watermarks, and no limits.

📖

Authentic RAQM Shaping

Every verse is rasterized via Pillow + HarfBuzz/RAQM using KFGQPC Uthmani Hafs v18 so diacritics (tashkeel) render crisply.

🎙️

Inter-Ayah Pause Engine

Detects silent pauses strictly between the end of one Ayah and the start of the next so text dissolves calmly and never flickers mid-ayah.

🎞️

Seamless Loop & Audio

Source recitations are never speed-stretched. Video backgrounds crossfade seamlessly across loops at 720×1280 24fps H.264/AAC.
